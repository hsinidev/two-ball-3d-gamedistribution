### Game Details & Guide
Two Ball 3D Gamedistribution



Game Overview


Two Ball 3D Gamedistribution


This browser-based game challenges players to roll three balls into a hole on a rotating wheel.


Objective: Reach the hole by rolling the balls in the correct order and without missing any.



How to Play



Controls:






Up Arrow (move left)




Down Arrow (move right)




Rotate Wheel




Pause Game






Objective:





Roll the balls in a specific order to reach the hole.


Missing any ball will result in losing turns.





Gameplay Sequence


Here's an overview of the gameplay sequence:
  

    
Start by rolling the first ball onto the wheel.

    
The second and third balls must be rolled on top of each other in a specific order (e.g., red, green, blue).

    
Rotate the wheel to align the balls with the hole.

  




Tips and Tricks for Success



Rolling Strategy:





Focus on rolling a sequence of three consecutive colors (e.g., red, green, blue) before switching to different colors.


Aim for the center of the wheel to increase your chances of aligning with the hole.






Adapting to Opponents:





Watch out for opponents who are trying to disrupt your sequence by rolling balls in an unexpected order.


Avoid blocking other players' attempts to steal turns or disrupt their sequences.





Why You Should Play It



Improved Problem-Solving Skills:



You'll develop your spatial reasoning and problem-solving skills as you navigate the game.



Enhanced Strategic Thinking:



You'll need to think critically about the sequence of balls and the wheel's rotation to succeed.
